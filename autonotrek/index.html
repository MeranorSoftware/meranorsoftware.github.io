Current development focus

The platform starts with a modular scanning brain.

AutonoTrek is carrier-independent by design. The current engineering sequence begins with the MSB-1, validates operator-guided collection through Hand, and develops the Control software needed to review and manage missions.

Current focus

MSB-1

The modular scanning brain: sensing, capture, compute, evidence, and connection architecture in one transferable mission unit.

Current focus

AutonoTrek Hand

The first practical carrier. It gives an operator a controlled way to validate the MSB-1, workflows, ergonomics, and evidence model.

Software prototype

AutonoTrek Control

Mission planning, readiness, device state, map context, scan review, and uncertainty handling in a shared operational interface.

The operating loop

Move. Scan. Verify.

  1. 01

    Map

    Establish approved areas, rack identities, routes, obstacles, scan positions, and a versioned warehouse context.

  2. 02

    Move

    Guide an operator or future carrier through approved collection work using deterministic mission rules.

  3. 03

    Scan

    Capture barcode, visual, and location evidence without fabricating exact identifiers.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Preserve confidence, uncertainty, supporting evidence, and human review before results become operational truth.

Non-negotiable principles

01

Evidence first

02

Human authority

People approve missions, define boundaries, and resolve uncertainty.

03

Exact data stays exact

Barcode identifiers and authoritative records are captured—not guessed by AI.

04

Carrier independent

The intelligence platform is not permanently tied to one robot or form factor.
