About Meranor Systems

Build systems that make difficult work clearer.

Meranor is an independent software, AI, and technology company focused on practical operations—especially where physical inventory, business records, and human decisions meet.

The mission

Turn complexity into something people can see, understand, and act on.

Meranor exists to build technology that respects the reality of the work. That means showing uncertainty instead of hiding it, preserving the evidence behind decisions, and designing systems around the people accountable for the outcome.

Founder

Built from the operations floor outward.

Meranor was founded by Josh Welch, an inventory and operations professional who began designing software around the friction he saw in real workflows: disconnected records, difficult counts, unclear ownership, and technology that often asks the operator to adapt to it.

The company’s product direction grows from that perspective—part operator, part builder, and relentlessly curious about how the system could work better.
