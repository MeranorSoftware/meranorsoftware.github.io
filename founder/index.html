Founder of Meranor Systems

Josh Welch

Inventory professional, hands-on builder, and relentlessly curious problem-solver turning operational friction into systems worth using.

Operator turned builder

Meranor started with a simple question: why does the system make the real work harder?

Josh founded Meranor after years of working close to inventory, purchasing, warehouse processes, and the people accountable when the records and physical reality do not agree. The product ideas began as practical responses to that friction—not as technology looking for a problem.

AutonoTrek grew from the challenge of collecting trustworthy inventory evidence in physical environments. UnoSolu grew from the broader need to connect the records, decisions, and teams surrounding that work.

“Build the thing that would have made the work clearer, then make sure the people doing the work remain in control.”

The builder’s rules

Curious enough to imagine it. Practical enough to prove it.

01

Start with the operator

Understand the real workflow, constraints, and accountability before designing the elegant solution.

02

Make uncertainty visible

A trustworthy system admits what it does not know and preserves the evidence needed to resolve it.

03

Build in honest stages

Concept, prototype, validation, pilot, and release are different achievements—and should stay different.
