What it does

Simple inputs. Better context. Cleaner decisions.

Tiny Portfolio is designed as a personal portfolio companion for structured reflection and ongoing visibility. It helps users capture what matters, preserve decision context, and stay grounded.

01

Guided check-ins

Focused prompts capture value, holdings, contributions, milestones, and the current portfolio call without turning the workflow into a chore.

02

Screenshot assist

Screenshot-based updates help turn visual portfolio snapshots into cleaner records and more useful review context.

03

Decision support

The experience organizes thinking around practical signals such as HOLD, WAIT, and REVIEW rather than impulsive short-term reactions.

04

Portable records

The direction centers user-controlled records that preserve milestones, reasoning, and the story behind each update.

Product direction

Clear, calm, and useful by design.

Tiny Portfolio is being shaped as a lightweight companion rather than a noisy fintech dashboard or an automated trading system.

Illustrative interface · Not live account data

How the workflow works

A straightforward personal portfolio rhythm.

The intended experience is simple: record updates, preserve context, review progress, and keep the long-term picture in view.

  1. 01

    Check in

    Capture the current snapshot through lightweight prompts or screenshots.

  2. 02

    Structure the update

    Organize holdings, progress, contributions, and milestone context into a clearer record.

  3. 03

    Review the call

    Reflect on the current posture using practical framing such as HOLD, WAIT, or REVIEW.

  4. 04

    Keep the story

    Preserve a portable history of milestones, rationale, and portfolio evolution over time.

Important note

Decision support, not trade execution.

Tiny Portfolio is a monitoring, journaling, and decision-support project. It does not execute trades, custody assets, guarantee outcomes, or replace professional financial advice.
