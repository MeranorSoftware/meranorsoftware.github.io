Connected operating system

One context across the business.

UnoSolu is intended to unify operational domains without flattening the details each team needs to do its job.

CRM

Customers & sales

Accounts, opportunities, quotes, orders, service context, and relationship history.

INV

Inventory & purchasing

Items, locations, replenishment, receiving, commitments, variances, and cost context.

OPS

Operations & projects

Work queues, ownership, milestones, exceptions, service, and cross-functional execution.

FIN

Finance & analytics

Operationally grounded reporting, reviewable explanations, forecasts, and performance.

Independent by default

UnoSolu does not require AutonoTrek.

The core platform is designed to operate as a complete business system on its own. Customers who license AutonoTrek can activate additional device, mission, warehouse-map, scan-ingestion, and reconciliation capabilities.

Platform rules

AI-native does not mean AI-unaccountable.

01

Records remain visible

Users can inspect the source records behind an answer or recommendation.

02

Actions are reviewable

Material changes should be prepared, explained, and confirmed before execution.

03

Workflows stay structured

Tables, forms, queues, and dashboards remain first-class—not buried behind chat.

04

Scale progressively

The platform should serve smaller teams cleanly while supporting deeper controls as organizations grow.
